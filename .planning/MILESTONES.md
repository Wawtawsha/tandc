# Project Milestones: Town & Country Furniture Website

## v1.0 Website Launch (Shipped: 2026-03-03)

**Delivered:** Complete local furniture store website for Town & Country in Farmville, VA with product showcases, trust-building content, contact/conversion paths, SEO, analytics, and Vercel deployment.

**Phases completed:** 1-8 (21 plans total)

**Key accomplishments:**

- Custom brand identity with oklch color system, Libre Baskerville + DM Sans typography, smooth scroll and page transitions
- Full product showcase with typed data layer for 10 La-Z-Boy and Ashley products, configurable via data files
- Video hero with YouTube facade pattern, trust signal strip, and six-category navigation grid
- About page with family-owned story, photo placeholders, local delivery messaging, and dual-brand partner badges
- Contact section with Google Maps embed, Zod-validated form with Resend email delivery, and directions CTA
- GA4 analytics tracking 8 event types across all interactions, with TRACKING_PLAN.md as data contract for Nessus
- WCAG AA accessibility (focus-visible indicators, semantic headings, alt text, skip-to-content, reduced motion)
- Mobile-first responsive with 48px touch targets, deployed to Vercel at https://tandc-six.vercel.app

**Stats:**

- 82 commits
