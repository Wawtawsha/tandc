    With `npm run dev` running, open http://localhost:3000 in your browser and check:

    **1. Color Palette (DSGN-01):**
    - Background should be warm ivory/cream (NOT white, NOT dark)
    - Text should be dark warm brown (NOT pure black, NOT light gray)
    - Navigation border should be barely visible, warm-toned
    - Overall feeling: warm, inviting, like a furniture showroom -- NOT corporate, NOT techy

    **2. Typography (DSGN-02):**
    - "Town & Country Furniture" heading should be in a SERIF font (Libre Baskerville -- has visible serifs, refined editorial feel)
    - Body text ("Farmville, Virginia's home for...") should be in a clean SANS-SERIF font (DM Sans -- geometric, modern)
    - The two fonts should feel complementary, not clashing

    **3. Smooth Scroll (DSGN-03):**
    - If the page is long enough to scroll, scrolling should feel smooth and momentum-based
    - Trackpad/mouse wheel scroll should have a slight inertia effect (Lenis)

    **4. Page Transitions (DSGN-04):**
    - Click "About" in the navigation bar
    - The page content should FADE OUT and SLIDE UP slightly, then the About page should FADE IN and SLIDE DOWN into place
    - The Navigation bar and Footer should NOT animate (they stay static during transition)
    - Click "Home" to go back -- same transition animation should play
    - If transitions are too fast to see, that is fine -- they default to 0.3s which is subtle

    **5. Navigation:**
    - Desktop: Logo "Town & Country" on left, "Home" and "About" links on right
    - Active page link should show in accent color (muted gold)
    - Hover over links should show subtle animation

    **6. Footer:**
    - Bottom of page shows copyright with "Town & Country Furniture. Farmville, Virginia."
    - No "Shrike" text anywhere
